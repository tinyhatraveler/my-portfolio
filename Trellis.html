Trellis:

Trellis is was originaly a test on how good claudes Fable 5 is at creating software with almost no guidance.

normaly i give photoshop edits of my projects and have claude talk me through the process so i can learn from it (eg this website) but with Trellis i just said:

"make me the best https://matrix.org/ client in the world. also please make it an app not a web app, a real windows app"



the result was alright, it was basic, didnt have end to end encryption or many of the other features i wanted. but it did work.

then we (me and Big F) asked for replying, profiles and member lists and most important of all; end to end encryption.

it took the rest of the tokens i had for the day but it worked well and it also was ported to mac and linux.

and after a few more requests, it got to this state.

we kept going on it and eventually it became a plugin for discord so my lazy ass could have one app instead of 2

this fable 5 really struggled with, it was interesting to see tbh.

it sits just below the dms but before the server list, and it works alright. it ported over most of the features, added google sign in and a third more evil feature (secret)

its decent but has a long way to go before i start using it regularly. but it is worlds above stable, and has been since the 3rd message i sent.



im still working on it, and am still shouting at claude to fix one specific bug that it cant seem to get its head round. but its getting there, and i might share it eventualy. but who knows

overall its been a great experiment for my 1 week of claude pro and i am very impressed with what it can do. but also at the same time with how moronic it is.
